(06) Approach

Four questions
before anyone opens
the timeline.

Taste starts discussions. Standards end them. Every clip we ship has to answer these — in writing, not in a call.

  1. Q1

    Does it hold the first two seconds?

  2. Q2

    Do you feel something?

  3. Q3

    Can the viewer use it tomorrow?

  4. Q4

    Is it one message — or three?

4/4 ships. 3/4 gets re-cut. 2/4 gets killed — with timecode and a reason.
