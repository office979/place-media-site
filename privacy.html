Visiting this website

When you open this website, the server of our hosting provider automatically processes the technical data your browser sends: IP address, date and time, the page requested, referrer, browser and operating system. This is necessary to deliver the website and keep it secure (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). These log files are deleted after a short period and are not combined with other data.

Fonts

All fonts are hosted on our own server. No connection to Google or any other font provider is made.

Inquiry form

When you send the inquiry form, we receive your name, email address, company, the type of project, budget range, timing and your message, together with the page you sent it from. We store this in a Google Sheet and send you an automatic confirmation email and our team a notification. We use this data only to answer your inquiry and, if it leads there, to prepare and carry out a project (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR, and Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR for our legitimate interest in answering inquiries).

The sheet and the emails are processed by Google (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) as our processor. Data may be transferred to Google LLC in the United States; Google is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. We delete inquiries that did not lead to a project after 24 months at the latest, and keep project correspondence for statutory retention periods.

Email and phone

If you write to us or call us directly, we process your details only to handle your request (Art. 6 (1) (b) and (f) GDPR).

YouTube videos

Behind-the-scenes videos are embedded in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you click play. Only then does your browser connect to YouTube, operated by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, and data — including your IP address — may be transferred to Google LLC in the United States. By clicking play you consent to this (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR). See Google's privacy policy.

Links to other websites

We link to Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube and our clients' websites. Data is only transferred to these services once you follow a link; their own privacy policies then apply.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. Just write to office@placemedia.at.

You can also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Austria, the Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, dsb.gv.at.
