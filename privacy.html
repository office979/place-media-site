Visiting this website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, United States. When you open the website, GitHub's servers automatically process the technical data your browser sends: IP address, date and time, the page requested, referrer, browser and operating system. This is necessary to deliver the website and keep it secure (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). We do not have access to these log files and do not combine them with other data. As GitHub is based in the United States, this data may be processed there; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Images and videos

All images and videos on this website are stored on the same server as the website itself. No content delivery network or third-party media service is involved. Videos only load once they come into view.

Fonts

All fonts are hosted on our own server. No connection to Google or any other font provider is made.

Inquiry form

When you send the inquiry form, your browser transmits your name, email address, company, the type of project, budget range, timing and your message, together with the page you sent it from, to a Google Apps Script web app (script.google.com). It stores the inquiry in a Google Sheet and sends you an automatic confirmation email and our team a notification. Nothing is transmitted before you press send. Name and email address are required to answer you; all other fields are voluntary. We use this data only to answer your inquiry and, if it leads there, to prepare and carry out a project (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR, and Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR for our legitimate interest in answering inquiries).

The sheet and the emails are processed by Google (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) as our processor. Data may be transferred to Google LLC in the United States; Google is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. We delete inquiries that did not lead to a project after 24 months at the latest, and keep project correspondence for statutory retention periods.

Email and phone

If you write to us or call us directly, we process your details only to handle your request (Art. 6 (1) (b) and (f) GDPR).

YouTube videos

Behind-the-scenes videos are embedded in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you click play. Only then does your browser connect to YouTube, operated by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, and data — including your IP address — may be transferred to Google LLC in the United States. By clicking play you consent to this (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR). See Google's privacy policy.

Links to other websites

We link to Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube and our clients' websites. Data is only transferred to these services once you follow a link; their own privacy policies then apply.

No automated decisions

We do not use automated decision-making or profiling.
